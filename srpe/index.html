Session RPE Report · 2026–27 Season

sRPE as a performance signal, not just a load log.

Session RPE is often collected and rarely read. This report takes every session the team has logged so far this season and renders it as weekly load trends, acute-vs-chronic ratios (using exponentially weighted moving average; EWMA), team-vs-coach comparisons, and — the piece most load reports stop short of — correlation between load and the countermovement rebound jump performance.

The question

If we're going to ask every athlete to report their perceived exertion after each session, we need to ensure that the information collected serves a meaningful purpose. Our goal was to answer two important questions: First, how does the team's training load fluctuate throughout the season, and can we identify periods of increased or decreased training demands? Second, how do these training loads relate to subsequent neuromuscular performance, as measured through our jump assessments? Ultimately, we wanted to move beyond simply monitoring training load and begin examining how the demands imposed on our athletes might influence their physical performance in the days that follow.

The data

The report consists of every logged session for the 2026–27 season to date, broken into focus, intensity (sRPE), load (sRPE × duration), and separated RPE legs / RPE lungs ratings so the report can distinguish neuromuscular from cardiovascular stress. Jump data is pulled from the parallel countermovement rebound jump testing protocol and aligned by date.

The approach

R Markdown report, rendered as a single interactive HTML file for the coaching staff. Each section is a sport-science-standard load metric (ACWR, EWMA, monotony & strain) rendered as a Plotly chart with the team and individual athletes selectable. The correlation sections use rolling windows at both the athlete-day and team-week level so the staff can see signal at the level they make decisions at.

What's in the report

  • Load vs Performance Correlation MatrixCorrelation between daily load metrics and next-day CMRJ performance, at both athlete-day and team-week resolutions
  • Cumulative Team LoadSeason-to-date totals

All athlete identifiers have been removed for public display. The report preserves the structure, visualizations, and analyses shared with the coaching staff.
