Session RPE Report · 2026–27 Season

Every logged session rendered as weekly load, acute-chronic ratios, and jump-performance correlations.

In a limited budget program, sports science can still be applied using principles that help in analyzing pertinent data and with tools that have been validated through research. Session RPE is often collected and rarely read. This report takes every logged session so far and renders it as weekly sRPE / RPE-legs / RPE-lungs trends, EWMA acute vs chronic load, ACWR, monotony & strain, AM vs PM paired analysis, and — the piece most load reports stop short of — correlation between daily load and next-day countermovement rebound jump performance.

Shiny Application · In production

Live Play Call Tracker — an iPad-first sideline tool.

A production R Shiny app the coaching staff opens on an iPad during practice and games. One tap per possession captures the play, result, ball reversals, paint touches, clock, quarter, and category — then the dashboard surfaces per-play rollups, cross-session trends, category tactical signatures, and practice-vs-game comparisons with 95% confidence intervals backed by Welch's t-test. A public interactive demo is live.
